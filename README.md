## 🌍 About The Project

**GridPulse BD** is an advanced explainable AI platform tailored specifically for Bangladesh. It provides critical insights into the nation's energy infrastructure by analyzing and visualizing complex data.

- 🔌 **Grid Stress Monitoring:** Real-time visualization and forecasting of power grid loads.
- ⚖️ **Rural–Urban Energy Equity:** Identifying disparities in energy distribution to ensure fair access.
- ☀️ **Renewable Energy Planning:** Data-driven approaches to integrate solar and wind energy effectively into the national grid.

## ✨ Features
- 🗺️ **Interactive Mapping:** Explore high-resolution, interactive energy maps powered by MapLibre.
- 📊 **AI-Powered Analytics:** Machine learning models that forecast demand and supply gaps.
- 🔐 **Secure Access:** Integrated authentication system for secure workspace operations and user-specific dashboards.
- ⚡ **High Performance:** Built on edge infrastructure ensuring lightning-fast load times.

## 🚀 Technologies Used
- **Frontend Framework:** Next.js (Vinext), React
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
- **Mapping & Geospatial:** MapLibre GL, BDAtlas
- **Backend & Database:** Drizzle ORM, Cloudflare D1
- **Tooling:** Vite, ESLint, TypeScript

## 📈 Impact
By providing an intuitive and accessible platform for energy data, GridPulse BD aims to assist policymakers, researchers, and engineers in making informed decisions to ensure a sustainable energy future for Bangladesh.
